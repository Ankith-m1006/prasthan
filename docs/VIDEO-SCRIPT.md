Runtime 02:31.00. The scene WAVs include their edit holds. In scene 4, mix the recorded Kannada app reply into the final six seconds. The master contains narration only.

## 01. The Sankranti rush

**Time** 00:00.00 to 00:14.14 (14.14 seconds)

**On screen** Start on the Bengaluru departures and the Hubballi rush warning. Hold on the date as the opening question lands.

**Voiceover** It’s the night before Sankranti, and everyone in Bengaluru wants to get home. A traveller can see which buses are running, but who tells the operator how many people still need a seat?

## 02. Meet the traveller

**Time** 00:14.14 to 00:26.15 (12.01 seconds)

**Voiceover** Meet our traveller, who lives in Bengaluru and has family in Hubballi. She sets her home region and chooses Kannada, so the app can speak to her the way her family does.
